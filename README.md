When something ships under my name, I want it to be something I'm proud of - and something that delivers real business value.

I've been called easy to work with and hard to ignore in a technical discussion - I'll take that. Whether that's collaboration with a designer about style guide consistencies, API optimizations with a backend colleague, helping the QA team shape testing strategies, or mentoring a junior through their first big refactor - I show up fully, with firm but approachable authority.

I've built and guided teams, **delegated effectively across skill levels**, managed client relationships across time zones, and learned that the best technical decisions almost always start with a good conversation. **I plan in outcomes, not tickets.** I'm naturally curious - if there's a performance win to be had or a process bottleneck slowing the team down, I'll find it and make the case for fixing it.

What I bring isn't just a strong technical foundation - it's the kind of seniority that shows up in **consistent delivery cadence** and how a team feels when things get hard. I've been the person who organizes the response when production breaks, who guides a teammate through a refactor they didn't think they could handle, and who keeps the conversation going with a client even when the news isn't great. I work hard to be someone people want in their corner.
